# Unclaimable.Extended data sources

Unclaimable.Extended embeds a deterministic snapshot and performs no runtime network requests.

- **Companies and derived financial/healthcare/media/transport organization names:** U.S. SEC `company_tickers_exchange.json` public reference data. The source of record is the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. A GitHub mirror of the SEC snapshot was used as transport during generation.
- **Regional brands:** FranchiseVS franchise dataset, CC BY 4.0, `jakeatthekeyboard/franchise-dataset`.
- **Education:** Hipo university-domains-list, MIT, `Hipo/university-domains-list`.
- **Sports:** OpenFootball clubs, CC0 1.0, `openfootball/clubs`.
- **Airports and administrative regions:** OurAirports open-data snapshot, public domain/Unlicense, `davidmegginson/ourairports-data`.
- Other categories are curated within Unclaimable from widely recognized public identities, titles and terms.

The optional 0.8.4 regional-role and crypto subgroups are small hand-curated name lists, checked against primary references in October 2026. Country-specific titles are drawn from [Rijksoverheid municipality and province descriptions](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/overheid-en-democratie/gemeenten/burgemeesters) and [USAGov state and local office descriptions](https://www.usa.gov/elected-officials). Wallet examples are checked against [ethereum.org's wallet directory](https://ethereum.org/wallets/find-wallet). Crypto group names are checked against the providers' own sites and documentation, including [Coinbase Exchange](https://www.coinbase.com/exchange), [Curve](https://docs.curve.finance/developer/documentation-overview/), [Lido](https://docs.lido.fi/earn/architecture/) and [Circle](https://www.circle.com/). These links identify names; no third-party textual datasets are imported. Availability and naming can change, so consumers should review their own risk profile.

Core data remains authoritative: Core entries are indexed before Extended entries and are never replaced by an Extended collision.

Names and marks remain the property of their respective owners. Inclusion is descriptive and does not imply affiliation or endorsement.
